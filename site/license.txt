Public MRI demonstration and feature images
------------------------------------------
OpenNeuro ds007401 / IDEAS II, subject sub-4082, session 1, T1w and FLAIR.
Dataset: https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds007401/versions/1.0.0
License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Publisher metadata:
https://github.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds007401/blob/2e7f2573a5f8a9921cb19fca5e601bc141633f49/dataset_description.json
Reference: Taylor, Peter N., et al. The imaging database for epilepsy and surgery
(IDEAS). Epilepsia 66.2 (2025): 471-481. https://doi.org/10.1111/epi.18192

Files: demo/index.html, assets/hero-volume.jpg, assets/hero-volume.json,
assets/feature-views.jpg, assets/feature-measures.jpg, assets/feature-notes.jpg,
assets/feature-masks.jpg and assets/feature-mcp.png.
Modifications: alignment, brain masking, display resampling, compression and
viewer screenshots. Notes, measurements and masks are demonstration drawings,
not expert lesion labels or AI findings. Dataset-author endorsement is not implied.
Source and derivative checksums: demo/source-record.json and demo/artifacts.json.
